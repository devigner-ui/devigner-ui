# Four new components
Version: 1.7.0

A growth chart with rolling digits, a badge for every kind of label, a frame for images that are still being made, and a slider whose fill glows with drifting dust.

- NEW kpi-chart: A growth curve that draws itself in, with a dot you click along the line and a card whose digits roll to the change.
  - **Rolling digits:** only the digits roll, one wheel per place, while the sign and % hold still
  - **Glide to a point:** click, tap or drag and the dot travels along the curve to that month
  - **Trend chip:** an arrow turns and slides in when the change flips from rising to falling
  - **Any currency:** `currency` takes an ISO code and the locale places the symbol
  - **Honest curve:** monotone, so growth data never shows a fake dip
  - **Keyboard ready:** a slider with arrow keys, Home and End, and the point read out
  - **One accent:** the text color of `classNames.chart` colors the line, dot and fill
- NEW badge: A small label pill for statuses, filters, people and presence, tinted from one text color.
  - **Fold away:** `onRemove` folds the badge shut and its neighbours slide over
  - **One color:** fill and outline come from the text color, so any text class makes a tone
  - **Five tones:** neutral, success, warning, danger and info, readable in both themes
  - **Avatars and flags:** `media` fits an image to a circle sized to the badge
  - **Link or button:** `href` or `onClick` makes the whole pill clickable, and `linkAs` takes Next's Link
  - **Focus kept:** after a remove, focus moves to the next badge
  - **Three sizes:** `size` sets 24, 28 or 32px
- NEW image-generation: A frame for an image that is still being made, where a grid of tiny dots takes on the picture's colors and dissolves into it.
  - **Tinted dots:** while refining, each dot takes the color of the image under it
  - **Swell and ripple:** the dots swell around the cursor and a click sends a ripple through them
  - **Five states:** queued, generating, refining, complete and error, each written out for screen readers
  - **Retry on error:** `onRetry` adds a Try again button under the frame
  - **Download when ready:** `download` puts a download button in the corner once the image is complete
  - **Your own media:** pass a Next Image or video as children, and `tintSrc` a small thumbnail for the colors
  - **Any shape:** `aspectRatio` and `focus` crop the frame and keep the subject in view
- NEW stardust-slider: A slider in a labelled card whose fill glows with drifting dust, with a soft handle that firms up when grabbed and a readout whose digits roll to the value.
  - **Drifting dust:** white motes drift and twinkle in the fill, gathering toward the handle
  - **Stretchy handle:** the handle brightens when grabbed and stretches with the speed of the drag
  - **Rolling readout:** the number rolls digit by digit like an odometer, and a click lets you type a value
  - **Snap to marks:** `snap="marks"` pulls the fill onto labelled marks like detents
  - **Any number format:** `locale` and `formatOptions` write percents, currencies and grouped numbers
  - **Log scale:** `scale="log"` spreads small values out, or pass your own mapping
  - **Vertical and bare:** `orientation` and `variant` turn it into a tall fader or the track alone
  - **Keyboard ready:** arrow keys, Page Up and Down, Home and End, with the value read out
- UPDATED: Component requests show their status as a badge with an icon.
