# Four new interface components
Version: 1.9.0

A command palette, a right-click menu, a Dynamic Island with ten live views, and a Liquid Glass popover that holds anything.

- NEW command-palette: A search field over your commands, with nested pages that slide in and a highlight that glides between rows.
  - **Nested pages:** a row with `items` opens a page of its own, and Backspace or the back button steps out
  - **Gliding highlight:** one highlight follows the pointer and the arrow keys between rows
  - **Descriptions and actions:** rows take a `description` and an `action` word, and both are searched
  - **Group actions:** `groupActions` puts a button such as Clear on a group heading
  - **Dialog ready:** `autoFocus` and `onEscape` close a palette opened from a shortcut
  - **Key hints:** `hints` shows Move, Open and Back under the list
  - **Every string:** `messages` replaces the placeholder, empty states and hints
- NEW context-menu: A right-click menu that opens where you click, stays inside the screen and slides into submenus.
  - **Opens anywhere:** right-click, Shift+F10 or a long press on touch
  - **Stays on screen:** the menu moves inside the viewport instead of spilling off an edge
  - **Submenus:** a row with `items` slides into a menu of its own
  - **Shortcuts and icons:** rows show an `icon` and a `shortcut` key cap
  - **Destructive rows:** `destructive` marks a row like Delete in red
  - **Gliding highlight:** one highlight moves between rows as you point or use the arrow keys
- NEW dynamic-island: A black pill that springs open into live activities, with ten ready-made views from a timer to AirDrop.
  - **Ten presets:** Ring, Timer, Record, Music, AirDrop, AirDrop mini, Low battery, Call, Find My and Screen record
  - **Fitted springs:** opens, pops and snaps back on springs measured from iOS
  - **One shape:** the island sizes itself from its content and moves as a single layout transform
  - **Hop between views:** `hop` rests as the pill for a moment before opening the next view
  - **Floating:** `floating` pins it to the top center of the screen
  - **Announced:** each view's `label` is read out when it opens
- NEW morphing-popover: A Liquid Glass pill that swells into a panel holding anything: a form, a picker, a note.
  - **Holds anything:** pass any content, or a function that gets `close` for Cancel and Save buttons
  - **Two kinds of glass:** the lens that bends the page, or a WebGL shader over an image with `backdrop`
  - **Tint and blur:** `transparency` sets the tint and `blur` softens the shader glass
  - **Soft bounce:** it swells open and melts back on a gentle 1s spring
  - **Reduce Transparency:** the glass turns into a solid tint when the user asks for it
