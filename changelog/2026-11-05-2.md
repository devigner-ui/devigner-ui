# Rating, Flick Cards and Menu Notch
Version: 1.9.0

Three new marketing pieces, a shader option for Liquid Glass, and a round of polish across the library.

- NEW rating: Star rating with one fill that slides across every star, so half steps and pointer previews come free.
  - **Half stars:** `step` of 0.5 allows halves
  - **Live preview:** the fill follows the pointer before you pick
  - **Clearable:** `clearable` resets to 0 when the current rating is picked again
  - **Read only:** `readOnly` shows an average without taking input
  - **Any shape:** `icon` swaps the star for your own shape
  - **Form ready:** `name` submits the rating with the form
- NEW flick-cards-slider: A fanned deck of photo cards you flick sideways, the next card swinging to the front with one elastic overshoot.
  - **Flick to browse:** drag the front card sideways and the next one swings forward
  - **Fanned deck:** side cards fan out and dim behind the front card
  - **Late button:** the front card's `action` fades in last, as a link or a button
  - **Keyboard:** arrow keys move through the deck
  - **Controlled:** `index` and `onIndexChange` drive the front card
- NEW menu-notch: A pill with a menu toggle and a theme button that unfolds into a panel of grouped links.
  - **Unfolds open:** the pill grows into a panel of grouped `sections`
  - **Icon to X:** the menu icon crosses into an X as it opens
  - **Letter blur:** the toggle label blurs in letter by letter
  - **Rolling links:** every link rolls to its hover color
  - **Socials row:** `socials` adds a row of icon links at the bottom
- UPDATED: Liquid Glass and Morphing Popover can draw their glass with a WebGL shader over an image through the new `backdrop` and `blur` props.
- UPDATED: Chart Card, File Upload and Date Range Picker now share one look, a header on a soft base with the content on a raised card.
- UPDATED: Command Palette, Context Menu and Tabs share one interface style, and the Menu Dock border is lighter.
- UPDATED: The components sidebar is split into Interface and Marketing.
