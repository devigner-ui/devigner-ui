# Liquid Glass is here
Version: 1.5.0

A glass menu whose rim bends the page behind it, and component pages that respond faster.

- NEW liquid-glass: A glass menu pill that swells into a squircle panel of rows and melts back once you pick one.
  - **Bending rim:** in Chrome and Edge the rim refracts the page behind it, with a thin rainbow fringe like real glass
  - **Swell and melt:** the pill grows from its center into the panel while its label zooms out and the rows zoom in
  - **Elastic press:** the glass swells under a press and wobbles back when you let go
  - **Tint control:** `transparency` runs from ultra clear at 0 to fully tinted at 1
  - **Full keyboard:** arrow keys, Home and End walk the rows, Escape and Tab close the panel
  - **Controlled or not:** `open`, `defaultOpen` and `onOpenChange`
  - **Reduce Transparency:** the glass turns solid under the system setting, the way iOS does
  - **Squircle corners:** gentle squircle ends on the pill, fuller squircle corners on the open panel
- FIXED file-upload: The AI prompt and the llms.txt page now import `FileUploader`, the component their example renders.
- UPDATED: Component pages have new interface sounds, built on the Kobra sound kit.
- UPDATED: The code drawer on component pages opens at once instead of after a pause.
- FIXED: The tooltips on the previous and next arrows no longer run off the edge of the screen.
