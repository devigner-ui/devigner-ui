# Four new components
Version: 1.6.0

A glass menu, a copy button, a contact line you can copy piece by piece, and a navigation bar made of notches.

- NEW liquid-glass: A glass menu pill that swells into a squircle panel of rows and melts back once you pick one.
  - **Bending rim:** in Chrome and Edge the rim refracts the page behind it, with a thin rainbow fringe like real glass
  - **Swell and melt:** the pill grows from its center into the panel while its label zooms out and the rows zoom in
  - **Elastic press:** the glass swells under a press and wobbles back when you let go
  - **Tint control:** `transparency` runs from ultra clear at 0 to fully tinted at 1
  - **Full keyboard:** arrow keys, Home and End walk the rows, Escape and Tab close the panel
  - **Reduce Transparency:** the glass turns solid under the system setting, the way iOS does
- NEW copy-button: Copies text in one click, then turns green and retypes its label to Copied.
  - **Retyped label:** letters the two labels share stay put and only the differing ones swap
  - **Async values:** `value` can return a promise, and the button shows a spinner until it resolves
  - **Every state:** idle, pending, copied and error, each with its own label for translation
  - **Three sizes:** `size` sets 36, 44 or 52px, and `iconOnly` keeps the label for screen readers
  - **Custom copy:** swap `copy` to write rich content or use a fallback
- NEW contact-lens: A dim contact line with an icon row where hovering an icon lights its part of the line under a label, and a click copies it.
  - **Lit parts:** each icon lights its own piece of the line, or the whole line
  - **Floating pill:** a label above or below the lit part turns to Copied after a click
  - **Copy or link:** an item with `href` becomes a link instead of a copy
  - **Keyboard ready:** Tab lights each part like a hover, Enter or Space copies it
  - **Scales with text:** sized in em, so one text size in `className` scales it all
- NEW nav-notch: A row of small notches that works as a navigation bar, where the notch under the pointer opens into a labelled chip and its neighbours swell around it like a lens.
  - **Lens effect:** the chip follows the pointer and the notches either side grow taller and brighter
  - **Long glide:** the current mark travels between notches without bouncing
  - **Real links:** a nav landmark of links, or buttons for items without `href`, with `aria-current` on the current one
  - **One tab stop:** arrow keys, Home and End move inside the bar
  - **Vertical bar:** `orientation` and `side` stack the notches in a column with the chip popping out sideways
