# Three new components
Version: 1.8.0

Pill tabs with a sliding indicator, a button whose label flips on hover, and an icon bar whose tiles open to their labels.

- NEW tabs: A row of pill options inside a hairline ring, where a soft pill slides behind the one you pick.
  - **Sliding pill:** a soft pill glides behind the option you pick and never drifts when a parent scrolls or resizes
  - **Native radios:** arrow keys, focus and form submission work like any radio group
  - **Icons in options:** an option can carry an `icon`, and `label: null` shows the icon alone
  - **Fill mode:** `fill` stretches the group to its container and splits it into equal options
  - **Three sizes:** `size` sets 24, 28 or 32px
  - **Form ready:** `name` submits the selected value with the form
  - **Disabled options:** single options can be disabled, and the arrow keys skip them
- NEW flip-button: A pill button whose label flips up on hover while the icon in its badge slides out and back in.
  - **Flip on hover:** the label flips up as a copy rolls in, and the button never changes width
  - **Rolling badge:** the icon in the disc slides out and back in, or turns with `iconMotion="rotate"`
  - **Six variants:** primary, secondary, outline, glass, inverse and ghost, all from theme tokens
  - **Flip from state:** `flipped` holds the second label, such as Copied after a click
  - **Links too:** `asChild` gives a link or Next's Link the button's look
  - **Loading state:** `loading` puts a spinner in the badge and keeps the width
  - **Tunable roll:** `--flip-duration` and `--flip-stagger` set the timing
  - **Icon buttons:** sizes from sm to xl, plus two icon-only squares
- NEW expand-tabs: A row of square icon tiles for navigation, where the one under the pointer widens, fills with the accent and trades its icon for its label.
  - **Open on hover:** the tile under the pointer widens and its icon blurs into its label
  - **Badge dot:** `badge` puts a dot on a tile's corner, cut out of the tile and read by screen readers
  - **Links or buttons:** an item with `href` renders a link, otherwise a button that runs `onSelect`
  - **Any accent:** `color` sets the open tile and the dot
  - **Selected tab:** `value` marks a tile with `aria-current` and moves the dot onto it
  - **Keyboard focus:** tabbing onto a tile opens it with a focus ring
- FIXED menu-dock: Sliding pills inside a scrolling panel no longer glide in from the wrong place when the dock resizes.
- NEW: Component pages have a full screen mode (F) that hides the sidebar and brings back the previous and next arrows.
- UPDATED: New components carry a redesigned New badge.
