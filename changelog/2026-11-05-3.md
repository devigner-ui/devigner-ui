# Liquid Glass gets a shader
Version: 1.9.0

Liquid Glass can now bend an image through a WebGL shader, and three components get a lighter, layered look.

- UPDATED liquid-glass: The glass can now be drawn by a WebGL shader over an image, as well as by the lens that bends the page.
  - **Shader glass:** `backdrop` takes an image and the element it fills, and the glass bends the patch under it
  - **Adjustable blur:** `blur` sets how far the shader softens the image
  - **Tinted for contrast:** `transparency` tints the shader glass too, so labels stay readable over a busy photo
  - **Every browser:** the shader works wherever WebGL does, not only in Chromium
  - **Softer motion:** the panel swells open and melts back on a gentle 1s spring
- UPDATED chart-card: The header now sits on a soft base and the total and bars on a raised card, matching the Date Range Picker.
- UPDATED file-upload: The panel is now a raised card on a soft base like the Date Range Picker, with a white Cancel button beside Submit.
- UPDATED menu-dock: The dock's border is much lighter, so its edge is barely there.
