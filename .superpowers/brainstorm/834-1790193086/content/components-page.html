Hovering a row, or tabbing onto it, loads that component onto the sticky stage. Clicking the row opens its docs page. Search matches names, descriptions and categories as you type, and "/" focuses the box from anywhere on the page. Category counts and the total come from the registry. "new" marks anything added in the last 14 days. The list is plain links in the server-rendered HTML, and the only JavaScript is the filtering and the one live stage.
