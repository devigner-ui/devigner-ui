Cards: one rule for every component. Each has the same 380px stage, and any component that is bigger is scaled to fit, still fully usable, so nothing needs a hand-made layout. The stage replaces the grid with a recessed surface and a soft light from above, and on hover a faint spotlight follows the pointer across it. Below the stage: name, one-line description, an arrow to the docs, and the install command. Scale: the page renders every card's text as HTML for search engines, but a component only starts running when its card scrolls near the viewport, and stops again far off screen. The toolbar filters and sorts without reloading. Footer: your current direction polished. The giant wordmark is pressed into the page, and its letters dip in a small bow under the pointer, which echoes the hero.
