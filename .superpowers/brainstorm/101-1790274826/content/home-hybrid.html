A plus B: two ways to merge them

Both keep the design-canvas idea from B and A's inspector, and both lead into the handoff section and the same SaaS order below: works-with strip, live bento, install, pricing, FAQ, then the footer with the giant wordmark. The difference is how the first screen is framed.

A+B 1

The canvas lives inside the window Recommended

A's layout: headline on top, product window below, tilted back until you scroll. Inside the window is a small design tool: layers on the left, a draggable canvas of live frames in the middle, and the inspector on the right. Click a layer or a frame and everything follows it, including the install command docked under the canvas.

A+B 2

The whole screen is the canvas

B's layout: the hero is a full-bleed canvas with the headline written on it. A's inspector appears as a floating panel when you select a frame, holding the name, description, theme and motion toggles, keyboard map and install command. The bottom toolbar keeps only the tools.
